Testing the Pseudo-Dirac Nature of Atmospheric Neutrinos

Olivia M. Bitter, André de Gouvêa, Srubabati Goswami, Supriya Pan

hep-ph hep-exarXiv:2611.07281

DUNE atmospheric neutrinos can constrain pseudo-Dirac mass splittings $\Delta m^2_{33'}\gtrsim 10^{-4}\text{ eV}^2$, with sensitivity strongly governed by sub-GeV detector angular resolution and correlations with standard oscillation parameters.

Novel idea

Focuses on atmospheric neutrinos at a DUNE-like LArTPC to probe the least-constrained pseudo-Dirac mass splitting $\Delta m^2_{33'}$ associated with the third mass eigenstate. It systematically quantifies how sub-GeV angular reconstruction performance and standard oscillation parameter profiling ($\theta_{23}, \Delta m^2_{31}$) impact experimental reach.

Method

Uses a 3+1 active-sterile maximal-mixing formalism for pseudo-Dirac neutrino propagation through a 5-layer PREM Earth model. Simulated atmospheric flux (Honda Homestake solar minimum) and GLoBES $^{40}\text{Ar}$ cross sections are evaluated for a 200 kt\cdot yr DUNE exposure across measured energy and zenith angle bins, profiling over NuFit global fit uncertainties.

Key results

  • A 200 kt\cdot yr DUNE exposure achieves a $3\sigma$ sensitivity limit of $\Delta m^2_{33'} \ge 2.0 \times 10^{-4}\text{ eV}^2$ (dimensionless parameter $\epsilon \ge 4.0 \times 10^{-2}$) assuming a conservative $50^\circ$ sub-GeV angular resolution.
  • An optimistic $15^\circ$ sub-GeV angular resolution improves the $3\sigma$ reach to $\Delta m^2_{33'} \ge 6.0 \times 10^{-5}\text{ eV}^2$ ($\epsilon \ge 1.2 \times 10^{-2}$).
  • Profiling over uncertainties in $\theta_{23}$ and $\Delta m^2_{31}$ weakens the sensitivity due to correlations between $\epsilon$ and the atmospheric disappearance amplitude.
  • Atmospheric neutrinos provide very weak standalone sensitivity to solar parameters $\Delta m^2_{21}$ and $\sin^2\theta_{12}$, failing to rule out $\Delta m^2_{21}=0$ at $>2\sigma$.

Why it matters to you

Directly relevant to DUNE atmospheric oscillation analyses, low-energy LArTPC detector performance requirements, and BSM pseudo-Dirac neutrino searches, connecting event-by-event angular resolution to mass-splitting constraints.

Caveats

Assumes maximal active-sterile mixing ($\theta_{34}$ determined solely by pseudo-Dirac structure) and sets $\delta_{\text{CP}}=0^\circ$; sensitivity relies heavily on sub-1 GeV angular reconstruction capabilities.

Abstract

Pseudo-Dirac neutrinos are characterized by extremely small mass-squared differences between two nearly degenerate, strongly-mixed, active-sterile states. These differences are associated with new very-long oscillation lengths and provide a probe of the nature of neutrinos. Atmospheric neutrinos can probe these oscillations as they allow access to a broad range of baseline distances, from tens of kilometers to the Earth's diameter. We investigate the sensitivity of an experiment like the Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment (DUNE) to the pseudo-Dirac nature of neutrinos through measurements of the atmospheric neutrino flux. We concentrate on the modifications to the atmospheric neutrino energy and angular spectra induced by one new oscillation frequency associated with the $\nu_3$ state and determine the parameter space that can be probed with atmospheric neutrinos at a DUNE-like detector. We also comment on the sensitivity of these measurements to the sub-leading "solar" oscillations, which are also characterized by a relatively small mass-squared difference.

A framework for matrix-element-corrected QED antenna showers in hadron decays

F. Abudinén, G. Morgante, P. Skands

hep-pharXiv:2611.07366

Presents a QED sector-antenna shower framework in Pythia/Vincia for hadron decays with full mass/spin dependence and automated matrix-element corrections up to $\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2)$.

Novel idea

Extends the Vincia antenna shower to generic hadron decays (spin-0, 1/2, 1) incorporating coherent soft-multipole eikonals and spin-dependent collinear DGLAP limits. It also establishes an automated FeynRules and MadGraph pipeline to introduce up to second-order tree-level matrix-element corrections (MECs).

Method

Formulates mass-dependent sector antennae in Vincia for QED radiation off charged hadrons. Incorporates tree-level ME corrections using an automated EFT toolchain, interfaces with EvtGen for hadron decay generation, and compares predictions against Photos, Pythia, and Sherpa/Photons++.

Key results

  • Second-order $\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2)$ ME corrections consistently increase average emitted photon energy by $1-3\%$ relative to uncorrected showers.
  • For fully charged three-body decays (e.g. $D^+ \to \pi^+\pi^-\pi^+$), the simplified maximal-screening dipole option overestimates average energy loss by $5-20\%$ compared to the full coherent multipole shower.
  • Demonstrates linear multi-threaded CPU speedup in EvtGen via thread-local Vincia instances, avoiding the thread-locking bottlenecks of Photos and Photons++.

Why it matters to you

Provides a modernized QED parton shower tool in Pythia/Vincia for hadron decay chains, offering useful insights into soft/collinear photon radiation modeling and generator multithreading efficiency.

Caveats

Hadronic form factors are currently set to unity (point-like approximation); spin-3/2 hadrons are treated as spin-1/2; tree-level MEC plugins are currently limited to scalar and vector decay channels.

Abstract

We present a general framework for the description of QED parton showers in hadron decays. The formalism is constructed in terms of coherent QED antennae with full dependence on particle masses and extends beyond the YFS approach by systematically incorporating spin-dependent (quasi)collinear single-unresolved limits to all orders. In addition, we incorporate corrections derived from tree-level matrix elements up to $\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2)$ and describe a generic procedure to obtain such matrix elements using FeynRules and MadGraph. We provide an explicit implementation of the method for decays involving pseudoscalar mesons and for decays of vectors and pseudoscalars into fermion-antifermion pairs, within the combined Pythia+Vincia parton-shower Monte Carlo framework, and compare the resulting QED emission spectra to those generated with Photos and Sherpa+Photons++ through the EvtGen generator.

Wigner distribution modeling of nuclear state in NuWro Monte Carlo event generator

Rwik Dharmapal Banerjee, Alexis Nikolakopoulos, Jan T. Sobczyk, Hemant Prasad, Krzysztof M. Graczyk, José L. Bonilla + 1 more

nucl-tharXiv:2611.07817

Develops a shell-resolved nuclear Wigner distribution initial state in NuWro for quasielastic scattering, handling negative quasiprobability regions via weighted and unweighted sampling and predicting measurable FSI effects.

Novel idea

Introduces quantum shell-resolved Wigner distributions (WDs) into a neutrino Monte Carlo event generator (NuWro), capturing position-momentum correlations of bound nucleons beyond the local Fermi gas approximation. It introduces two practical algorithms (signed weighted sampling and non-negative coarse-graining) to overcome negative WD values.

Method

Computes shell-resolved WDs from mean-field bound states for $^{12}\text{C}$, $^{16}\text{O}$, $^{40}\text{Ar}$, and $^{56}\text{Fe}$. Implements signed-branch sampling with event weights, as well as a coarse-graining algorithm that preserves the exact momentum marginal. Connects initial nucleon position/momentum to NuWro's primary vertex and intranuclear cascade (INC) FSI.

Key results

  • Inclusive cross sections in the WD framework strictly reduce to the plane-wave impulse approximation (PWIA) and remain non-negative despite negative initial WD regions.
  • Coarse-grained unweighted sampling introduces minimal spatial distortion ($\sim 3\%$ for $^{12}\text{C}/^{16}\text{O}$ and $\sim 7\%$ for $^{40}\text{Ar}/^{56}\text{Fe}$) while exactly reproducing the original shell momentum distribution.
  • Position-momentum correlations and shell-dependent interaction points shift the relative height of the $1p_{3/2}$ and $1s_{1/2}$ missing-energy peaks in JSNS2 mono-energetic $\nu_m\text{-}^{12}\text{C}$ interactions by $7-10\%$.

Why it matters to you

Directly impacts core neutrino-nucleus interaction modeling, MC event generators (NuWro, GENIE, GiBUU), initial-state nuclear structure implementation, and exclusive final-state predictions (INC FSI, missing energy in argon/carbon).

Caveats

Uses shell separation energies with delta-function binding energy assignments rather than realistic continuous spectral functions; initial nucleons are off-shell according to default NuWro kinematics.

Abstract

We develop a method to use the Wigner distribution (WD) description of the nuclear initial state of $^{12}$C, $^{16}$O, $^{40}$Ar, and $^{56}$Fe for quasielastic scattering in the NuWro Monte Carlo event generator. We propose two different algorithms to deal with the problem of negative values of the WDs. In our treatment, finite-nucleus WDs provide a correlation between the position and the momentum of bound nucleons that is very different from what is assumed in the standard approaches based on the Fermi gas model and spectral functions. We show that the use of WDs leads to effects that can be measured experimentally and may be relevant for future neutrino oscillation experiments.

Lattice QCD calculations of nucleon-nucleon scattering at $M_\pi \approx 200, 280\text{ MeV}$ matched to chiral EFT predict that the deuteron unbinds near $M_\pi \approx 220\text{ MeV}$.

Novel idea

Presents lattice QCD calculations of two-nucleon ($^1S_0$ dineutron and $^3S_1-^3D_1$ deuteron) scattering at light pion masses ($M_\pi \approx 200, 280\text{ MeV}$) analyzed via quantization conditions with explicit long-range one-pion exchange, enabling chiral EFT extrapolations of $NN$ observables to physical quark masses.

Method

Performs $N_f=2+1$ Wilson-clover lattice QCD simulations using distillation and multi-hadron operator bases. Extracts energy levels via generalized eigenvalue problems and maps them to scattering phase shifts using plane-wave and relativistic quantization conditions. Fits pion-mass dependence using Weinberg and KSW power-counting chiral EFTs.

Key results

  • Both Weinberg and KSW chiral EFT power countings predict that the deuteron becomes unbound near $M_\pi \approx 220\text{ MeV}$.
  • The pion-mass derivative of the inverse deuteron scattering length at the physical point is determined as $d(a^{-1}_{^3S_1})/dM_\pi = -0.47(16)$ (Weinberg) and $-0.46(24)$ (KSW).
  • Effective ranges $r_0$ for both $^1S_0$ and $^3S_1$ channels show very weak pion-mass dependence between $M_\pi \approx 138\text{ MeV}$ and $280\text{ MeV}$.

Why it matters to you

Provides crucial $ab\ initio$ inputs for nuclear chiral EFTs, lattice QCD nuclear forces, nuclear binding sensitivity to Standard Model parameters, and nuclear structure calculations.

Caveats

Neglects strange-quark mass variations across ensembles; KSW power counting displays a relatively large NLO/LO ratio $\mathcal{A}_0/\mathcal{A}_{-1}$, signaling potential scheme-dependent convergence challenges.

Abstract

Understanding how the properties of the visible Universe depend on the parameters of the Standard Model of particle physics requires \emph{ab initio} theoretical computations. To that end, we investigate the quark-mass dependence of low-energy nucleon-nucleon scattering in the deuteron and dineutron channels. We perform lattice-QCD calculations at pion masses $M_\pi\approx 200$ and $280$ MeV and compare their results to phenomenology at the physical point and to a previous calculation at $M_\pi\approx 710$ MeV. Scattering amplitudes, phase shifts, and threshold parameters are determined from finite-volume spectra using quantization conditions that explicitly incorporate long-distance effects from one-pion exchange. We analyze the pion-mass dependence of scattering lengths and effective ranges using chiral effective field theory with Weinberg and Kaplan-Savage-Wise power countings. From this, we estimate the pion-mass derivatives of the inverse scattering lengths and binding momentum, at the physical point. Both power countings indicate that the deuteron unbinds near $M_\pi\approx 220$ MeV.

hep-ph hep-exarXiv:2611.07067

Matches YFS soft-photon resummation with exact 1- and 2-photon matrix elements for $e^+e^-\to\pi^+ ext{\pi}^-(\gamma)$, achieving sub-0.04% generator agreement and introducing theory nuisance parameters for QED uncertainties.

Novel idea

Constructs amplitude-level YFS soft-photon exponentiation matched to exact NLO/NNLO-like real/virtual scalar QED matrix elements in two independent event generators (KKMC/CEEX and SHERPA/ACRAIC). It establishes a novel theory-nuisance-parameter framework to systematically estimate missing higher-order QED uncertainties.

Method

Models pions as sQED scalars dressed with the STRONG2020 pion form factor (F$\times$sQED vs. GVMD loop prescriptions). Computes complete 1-loop virtuals, 1-loop real-virtuals, and exact 1- and 2-photon tree amplitudes. Compares fiducial cross sections across five experimental setups (CMD, KLOE-I, KLOE-II, BESIII, B) and against BabaYaga@NLO.

Key results

  • KKMC and SHERPA fiducial cross sections agree to $\le 0.04\%$ at $\mathcal{O}(\alpha)$ and $\le 0.03\%$ at $\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2 L)$ across all experimental setups.
  • Theory nuisance parameter bands at $\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2 L)$ are $\sim 0.005\%$ for CMD, $\sim 0.1\%$ for KLOE-I, $\sim 0.4\%$ for BESIII, and $\sim 0.9\%$ for B-factories.
  • Using GVMD instead of point-like loops inside virtual diagrams shifts the forward-backward asymmetry in CMD-3 kinematics by up to $\sim 1\%$ near the $\rho$ peak.

Why it matters to you

Provides state-of-the-art QED radiative corrections and theoretical uncertainty quantification for $e^+e^-\to\pi^+\pi^-$, essential for hadronic vacuum polarization (HVP) input to $(g-2)_\mu$ and precision electron scattering analyses.

Caveats

Pion loop structure relies on effective sQED/GVMD form-factor modeling rather than full dispersive hadronic QED; non-logarithmic 2-loop initial-final boxes and $\mathcal{O}(\alpha^3)$ corrections are estimated via nuisance parameters rather than explicitly calculated.

Abstract

We present QED corrections to $e^+e^-\to\pi^+\pi^-(\gamma)$ in two independent Monte Carlo event generators, KKMC and SHERPA, for point-like scalar pions dressed with the pion form factor. All-order soft-photon exponentiation is combined with exact one- and two-photon tree amplitudes, the complete one-loop correction including the initial-final boxes with coherent interference, and at second order the photonic two-loop vertices and the exact one-loop correction to one-photon emission. At first order their fiducial cross sections agree to $\lesssim0.04\%$ and their distributions within statistical precision. The second-order band is $\sim0.005\%$ for CMD, $\sim0.4\%$ at $4$ GeV and $\sim0.9\%$ at $10$ GeV. Theory nuisance parameters estimate missing higher orders, and we construct a new rule for treating radiative return, where first-order corrections are of order one.
